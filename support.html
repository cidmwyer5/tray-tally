Common questions

The menu says "Sample menu" or "Couldn't update menu"

The app shows a built-in or saved menu when it can't reach the menu server, for example with no signal or before the day's menu is posted. Pull down on the Dining tab to try again.

The nutrition for a food looks wrong

Nutrition comes from Virginia Tech Dining Services and depends on the portion served, so it may not be exact. You can log a custom food from the Today tab.

How do I add a friend?

On the Social tab, tap Turn on friends, pick a name and agree to the community rules. Share your six-character friend code, or tap Add a friend and type theirs. You both show up as friends. Friends need to be signed in to iCloud on their iPhone.

What can friends see?

Your name, character, room, streaks, trophies, visits and cheers. Never your food, calories, weight or photos.

Friends say "Sign in to iCloud" or "Couldn't sync"

Open the iPhone Settings app and make sure you're signed in to iCloud, then pull down on the Social tab.

How do I block or report someone?

Open their room from the Social tab and tap ⋯. Blocking takes effect right away. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. To stop someone who has your code from seeing your room, choose ⋯ → Get a new code on your Friends card.

How do I delete my data?

To delete your friends profile: Social tab → Friends → ⋯ → Leave and delete my profile. To erase everything: Profile → Settings (gear icon) → Privacy → Reset all data. See the privacy policy.

Why won't my calorie target go lower?

The app won't set targets below 1,200 kcal (female) or 1,500 kcal (male), or plan toward a weight below a healthy BMI. Those limits are for your safety. Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian about your goals.
