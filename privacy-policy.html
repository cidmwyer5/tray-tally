Tray Tally Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE YOU PUBLISH]

Tray Tally ("the app") is a calorie, macro, water, weight and dining hall tracker with a character, a room and optional friends. This policy explains what information the app handles and what happens to it. The short version: your food logs, weight, body stats and photos stay on your device. If you turn on friends, only a friend-safe profile (your name, character, room, streaks and trophies) is shared, through Apple's iCloud. We never sell or share your data with advertisers.

Information that stays on your device

You may enter food logs, weight entries, water intake, workouts, height, age, sex, activity level, goals, dietary preferences, favorite and saved plates, foods you don't want recommended, and progress photos. Some of this is health information. It's stored only in the app's storage on your iPhone and is never sent to us, to your friends or to anyone else.

If you use iCloud Backup or an encrypted computer backup, your device backup may include this data. Those backups are handled by Apple under its own privacy policy. We can't access them.

Friends (optional)

Friends are off until you turn them on in the Social tab. When you do, the app stores a profile in the app's public iCloud (CloudKit) database, under a random six-character friend code. The profile contains:

  • the name you choose to show friends
  • your character's look, your room's style and earned decorations, pets and home level
  • streak lengths (days logging and hitting water, weeks with a workout), trophies earned and their tier
  • which days you logged in the last two months (dates only, no amounts), whether you worked out today and what kind, and how full today's water bottle is (on a 0 to 3 scale)
  • whose room you're visiting today

It never contains food, calories, macros, weight, body stats, weight-goal trophies or photos. The app also stores links between you and the friends you add, the cheers (emoji reactions) you send, and any reports you file about another person.

Anyone who has your friend code can see your profile. You can block people, get a new code (the old one stops working), or delete your profile at any time. The data is used only to show your profile to friends and to review reports. It isn't used for advertising or tracking and isn't shared with anyone else. Apple stores it as our service provider; your iCloud account is used to sign in, but we don't receive your Apple ID, email or name from it.

Information we collect

We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking. The only information that leaves your device is the optional friends profile, links, cheers and reports described above. The app doesn't use your location, contacts or Apple Health data. The camera and photo library are used only to add progress photos, which stay on your device.

Network requests

To show dining hall menus and hours, the app downloads the day's menu from our menu server (hosted on GitHub Pages) [and/or Virginia Tech Dining Services' menu system]. These requests contain only the date being requested. Like any web server, the host may briefly keep standard technical logs such as IP address. We don't use these logs to identify you, and nothing you've entered in the app is included in these requests.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification content is sent to us.

Deleting your data

  • Friends profile only: Social tab → Friends → ⋯ → Leave and delete my profile. Your profile, links and cheers are deleted from iCloud.
  • Everything: Profile → Settings (gear icon) → Privacy → Reset all data. This erases everything on your device and deletes your friends profile too.

Deleting the app removes the data on your device but doesn't delete a friends profile, so leave friends first. You can also email us your friend code and we'll delete it.

Children

The app is meant for college students. It isn't directed to children under 13, and the friends feature requires an iCloud account.

Health disclaimer

Tray Tally is for general wellness and information only. It isn't medical advice and doesn't diagnose or treat any condition. Calorie targets and goal estimates are approximate. Menus and nutrition come from Virginia Tech Dining Services and may not be exact. Tray Tally isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Virginia Tech.
