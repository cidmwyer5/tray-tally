By turning on friends in Tray Tally, you agree to these rules. They apply to the name you show friends and to everything you send through the app.

Zero tolerance

There's no place in Tray Tally for objectionable content or abusive users. Don't use a name, or send anything, that is:

  • hateful or discriminatory toward any person or group
  • sexual, explicit or otherwise inappropriate
  • threatening, harassing, bullying or encouraging self-harm
  • pretending to be another person
  • spam, advertising or illegal

Reporting and blocking

Open a friend's room and tap ⋯ to report or block them. Blocking hides them, their visits and their cheers right away. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. If someone breaks these rules, we remove their profile and may block their friend code from the service.

Your profile

Anyone with your friend code can see your profile. Only share it with people you know. You can get a new code or delete your profile at any time from the Social tab. See the privacy policy for exactly what's shared.

Health

Tray Tally is for general wellness, not medical advice. Don't pressure friends about food, weight or exercise.
